## Description (English)

You wake up, check your phone, and still have no idea what actually matters today.

Your goals live in Notion. Your workouts in Whoop. Your money in a spreadsheet. Your agenda in iCloud. None of it talks to each other. None of it tells you what to do.

Life Agent does.

It's a Python system that pulls your real data — recovery score, calendar, goals, portfolio — and runs it through six AI agents that argue, analyze and decide what your day should look like. Then it sends you a briefing before you open your laptop.

**What's inside:**

- Morning briefing: recovery + calendar + 3 AI focus points, ready when you wake up
- Evening check-in: commitments, progress, what you're pushing to tomorrow
- Weekly AI review with pattern analysis across all your data
- Goals tracker with deadlines and automatic progress nudges
- Journal with AI pattern recognition
- Multi-agent board: Planning, Sport, Money, Opportunities, Critic and Motivation debate your day
- Portfolio tracker with compound projections

Runs locally. Built in Python. No SaaS fees. No subscriptions. No third party with your data.
